🛡 Data Integrity, Methodology & Critical Assumptions
Primary history: official NSE security-level bhavcopy archive. 52W-high metric: unchanged from the supplied production methodology — highest closing price over the 252-session lookback. A/D: NSE average-price methodology. TRIN: corresponding advance/decline volume breadth. Breakout Follow-Through: T-day HIGH must exceed the highest HIGH of the preceding 55 completed sessions; confirmation is T-day CLOSE above that same prior-55-session high. No T+1/T+2 data is used. RVOL: 5-session average volume divided by the prior 20-session average volume × 100. Sector penetration: current NIFTY 500 industry classification layer. No synthetic fallback formulas are used.
Universe: the all-share breadth universe varies by daily securities available in the NSE bhavcopy. Cross-session comparisons therefore describe participation in the available market universe, not a fixed constituent panel.
Cap buckets: Large/Mid/Small are derived from the available daily market-cap ranking layer (Top 100 / 101–250 / 251–500), not fixed NIFTY index constituents.
27% return penetration: percentage of eligible stocks in each sector with a return of at least +27% over the latest 40 completed sessions; sectors are ranked high to low.
55-day high penetration: percentage of eligible stocks in each sector whose latest-session HIGH is at least 97% of the highest HIGH in the preceding 55 completed sessions; sectors are ranked high to low. Classification uses the current NIFTY 500 industry map, so historical classification changes are not reconstructed.
EMA breadth: computed from available security-level closes; it is a participation measure, not an index trend signal.
Expert view: the interpretation weighs breadth, volume, volatility, trend and leadership together, and explicitly displays contradictory evidence rather than forcing a bullish/bearish conclusion.
